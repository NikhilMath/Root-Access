## Book 3 Premise

A burned-out IT manager is basically a ghost at work. He finally gets on "happy pills," and suddenly he's calm, clear-headed and actually curious. For the first time, he notices the weird stuff he's ignored for years, like why one server always goes down at 3 a.m. He decides to finally figure out what's going on.

## The Filthy Conspiracy

The 3 a.m. server is secretly running the CEO's side business: a fetish site for people into office supplies (staplers, label makers, the works). It's actually what keeps the company afloat.

## Big Branch Point / Ending

Does he blow the whistle, or protect the weirdest cash cow in corporate history?

## R-Rated Flavor Ideas

- Unfiltered swearing: he finally says everything he's been thinking to users
- Too chill on the pills: flirting with the hot new vendor
- Office holiday party that goes off the rails
- Double-dose branch: trippy hallucination in the server room
